🏫 1. Project Background

Traditional library management often relies on manual processes to manage books, members, borrowing records and reservations.

📚 Manual Book Management

Book information may be recorded and managed manually, making it difficult to search and update.

📄 Paper Records

Borrowing and returning information may require paper-based records.

⏱️ Time Consuming

Staff may spend significant time checking books, members and borrowing records.

🎯 2. Project Objectives

📚 Manage Books

Add, update, delete and search library books.

👥 Manage Members

Store and manage member information.

🔄 Manage Borrowing

Manage borrowing and returning records.

🔖 Manage Reservations

Allow members to reserve unavailable books.

💰 Manage Fines

Calculate and track overdue fines.

📊 Generate Reports

Provide statistics and reports for library management.

📚 3. Project Scope

✅ In Scope

  • Book Management
  • Member Management
  • Book Borrowing
  • Book Returning
  • Book Reservation
  • Fine Management
  • Reports and Statistics
  • User Account Management

❌ Out of Scope

  • Online payment gateway
  • Physical library security system
  • Book delivery service
  • External bookstore management

⚠️ 4. Current Problems

📚 Problem 1 – Book Management

Difficulty tracking book information and availability.

👥 Problem 2 – Member Management

Member information can be difficult to organize and update.

⏰ Problem 3 – Overdue Books

Difficult to monitor overdue books and return dates.

💰 Problem 4 – Fine Management

Manual calculation of overdue fines can cause errors.

🔖 Problem 5 – Reservations

Difficult to track and manage book reservations.

🗄️ 5. Proposed Solution

The proposed Library Management System provides a centralized system for managing library information and operations.
